Pequod field note / second watch

Leviathan Log

A salt-dark dispatch from the rail, where the lanterns swing low and the white whale writes its name across the weather.

Captain's copy

  • The squall arrived before the bell, black-backed and muttering.
  • A pale back broke the water; every man forgot his breath.
  • Ahab smiled as if the storm had finally learned his language.
  • We lowered boats into a sea that wanted no witnesses.
  • By dawn, the horizon held one white scar and no apology.
